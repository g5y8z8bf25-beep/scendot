SCENDOT Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

SCENDOT (“SCENDOT”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information SCENDOT may collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you when you use the SCENDOT mobile application. By using SCENDOT, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Collect SCENDOT may collect the following categories of information when you use the app. Account and Contact Information We may collect: - Your name or display name - Your email address - A unique user or account identifier We use this information to create and maintain your account, authenticate you, associate content and activity with your account, communicate with you, and provide SCENDOT’s features. Location Information With your permission, SCENDOT may collect your precise location. Location information may be used to provide location-related functionality, such as discovering relevant Scenes, places, people, or activities based on location. You can control SCENDOT’s access to location information through your device settings. Some location-based features may not work properly if location access is disabled. Photos and Videos If you choose to upload photos or videos, SCENDOT may collect and store those files so that they can be displayed or used as part of your profile, Scenes, posts, or other user-created content. SCENDOT does not access your photo library unless you choose to grant the relevant permission or select media to upload. Messages and Communications SCENDOT may collect messages or communications that users send through features within the app. This information is used to provide messaging, conversations, Scene interactions, and other communication features. Other User-Generated Content SCENDOT may collect content you voluntarily create or submit, including Scene information, text, descriptions, posts, comments, replies, and other content associated with your use of the service. Content that you choose to make visible to other users may be displayed to those users according to the features and privacy settings available in SCENDOT. 2. How We Use Information We use collected information primarily to operate and improve SCENDOT, including to: - Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts - Provide Scenes, messaging, social, and location-related features - Display user-created content - Enable users to interact with other users and content - Maintain the security and integrity of the service - Prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access - Respond to user inquiries and support requests - Maintain and improve the reliability of SCENDOT We do not use the information described in this Privacy Policy for third-party targeted advertising. 3. Tracking and Advertising SCENDOT does not sell your personal information to data brokers. SCENDOT does not use the personal information described above to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes. If our advertising or tracking practices change in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and our App Store privacy disclosures as required. 4. How Information May Be Shared We may share information with service providers that help us operate SCENDOT, such as providers of hosting, databases, authentication, storage, communications, security, and other technical infrastructure. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to SCENDOT and subject to their applicable contractual and privacy obligations. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to: - Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request - Protect the rights, property, security, or safety of SCENDOT, our users, or others - Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our terms We do not sell your personal information. 5. Data Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate SCENDOT, provide requested features, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed. When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it unless applicable law requires us to retain it. 6. Account and Data Deletion Users may request deletion of their SCENDOT account and associated personal information. When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with that account unless we are legally required to retain certain information. Where user-generated content is associated with the account, we will handle deletion in accordance with applicable law and SCENDOT’s account-deletion process.
